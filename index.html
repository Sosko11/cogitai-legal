KIBER AVTOPILOT

Last updated: September 5, 2026

KIBER AVTOPILOT ("the App") is a personal, self-hosted publishing assistant used by its owner to prepare and upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own channels: YouTube (@CogitAIStudio) and TikTok (@cogitai). It runs on the owner's own computer; there are no other users and nothing is sold.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel and sets their title, description and tags.
  • Reads basic statistics of that same channel (views, likes) so the owner can review and schedule posts.
  • Uploads the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts or scheduled posts.

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
