KIBER AVTOPILOT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

KIBER AVTOPILOT ("the App") is a personal content tool used by its owner to post their own videos to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It has a single user: the owner.

What data the App handles

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts, stored locally on the owner's machine and used only to upload the owner's content and read basic account information.
  • Basic account and video metadata (channel name, video titles, view and like counts, posting options) returned by the YouTube and TikTok APIs, used only to display status to the owner.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's statistics. By using the App the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What is accessed: only the owner's own channel and videos (upload, update metadata, read statistics). The App does not access, download, scrape or display any other user's YouTube data.
  • Storage: the OAuth token and cached statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer and nowhere else. Cached statistics are refreshed at most daily and are kept no longer than 30 days.
  • Sharing: no YouTube data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Revoking access: the owner can revoke the App's access to their Google account at any time via the Google security settings page. Revoking access and deleting the local files removes all stored data.

What the App does NOT do

  • It does not collect data from any third parties or other YouTube/TikTok users.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

Storage and deletion

All tokens and data live on the owner's own computer. Revoking the App's access in YouTube/Google or TikTok settings and deleting the local files removes everything.
