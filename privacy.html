KIBER AVTOPILOT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

KIBER AVTOPILOT ("the App") is a personal content tool used by its owner to post their own videos to their own TikTok account.

What data the App handles

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's TikTok account, stored locally on the owner's machine and used only to upload the owner's content and read basic account info.
  • Basic account metadata (username, avatar, posting options) returned by TikTok's API, used only to display status to the owner.

What the App does NOT do

  • It does not collect data from any third parties or other TikTok users.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

Storage and deletion

All tokens and data live on the owner's own computer. Revoking the App's access in TikTok settings and deleting the local files removes everything.
